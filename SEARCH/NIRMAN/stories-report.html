2. How a question is answered

Post 2 · Thread: Process · Status: Process

This post describes the workflow only. Questions move from discovery to an answer, then to validation. Three types cover the routes: Type A is answered in one pass, Type B needs two discovery rounds, and Type C needs the question refined before it yields an answer. Validation uses five teams. If four agree, a person makes the final call. It has no questions or report links of its own.

6. What predicts cause importance and full-time action

Posts 6 and 7 · Thread: Text to ML · Status: Validated

A five-step discovery tracked 1,289 individuals. Each one-step rise in cause importance goes with about 35% higher odds of choosing full-time social work over part-time or monetary contributions alone. Five teams confirmed the result. It is a modest association, not proof of cause.

7. The Q1 discovery workflow

Post 8 · Thread: Text to ML · Status: Validated

Three steps from local discovery to validation. In one batch, the share wanting full-time more than doubled after the workshop, from about 1 in 5 to nearly 1 in 2. Where people start is the best clue to where they end up. Five independent checks agree, though who will change is still hard to predict.
