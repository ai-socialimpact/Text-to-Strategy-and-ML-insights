  • Stated intent is not a real career. "Full-time social career" at Post is what someone says right after the workshop, not a verified outcome.

  2. Re-run the analysis in small stages, one step at a time, each returning its results. Long all-in-one scripts have failed to return output.
  3. First check how complete each field is among matched Pre/Post pairs, and fix the batch-10 issue.
  4. Then test which fields move from Pre to Post and which relate to switching to full-time.
  5. Only then finalise a shortlist, ideally fewer than 20 fields.
