4 4. Answer Contextualized by Schema & Domain Model
How the Project Schema Explains & Validates This Answer:

The question is a single-table audit and breakdown on prod_nirman_demographics. That table is the NIRMAN participant registry, with one row per Participant_Id and 2,890 rows overall. NIRMAN is SEARCH's youth social-leadership programme. The question isolates female participants (Gender = 'FEMALE') in cohort Workshop_Batch_Number = 14 and asks how the 81 girls split across the Educational_Stream_Category rollup. The rollup has four labels: Medical, Engineering, Arts/Commerce/Science/Pharmacy and Other. All five runs agree on the result. 52 of 81 girls (64.20%) are in the Medical category, 19 (23.46%) in Arts/Commerce/Science/Pharmacy, 9 (11.11%) in Engineering and 1 (1.23%) in Other. Mean Age_At_Workshop is 22.15 for Medical, 23.78 for Engineering, 21.58 for Arts/Commerce/Science/Pharmacy and 20.00 for Other. No row has a null, blank or off-label category. No row needed remapping. No girl has an unknown age (age 0 means unknown in this schema). The Educational_Stream cross-check is clean. All 52 Medical girls carry 'Medical / Public Health', all 9 Engineering girls carry 'Engineering', the 19 rollup girls split into Arts 8, Science 8 and Commerce 3, and the single Other girl carries Law. In domain terms, the Batch 14 female cohort is predominantly medical or public-health students or graduates, and it sits at typical early-20s age. This is a descriptive registry fact about who attended. It says nothing about outcomes and does not show that the workshop caused anything. No join to the questionnaire table was needed or performed, so the grain stays at participant level and the N of 81 is not distorted by Pre/Post row multiplicity or by participants missing from either table.

Schema Entity / Table Role in Answer Relevance & Meaning
prod_nirman_demographics The only table used. Grain is 1 row per Participant_Id (unique), so unique-ID counts equal row counts and the 81 baseline is a clean participant count. It supplies the cohort filter (Workshop_Batch_Number), the gender filter, the education rollup being audited, the detailed stream used for the cross-check, and the age at the first (Point-1) workshop. All filter and breakdown columns except Educational_Stream sit in the schema's 'usable' tier (no missing values). That is why the audit found no nulls or blanks and why the Educational_Stream_Category counts add to 81 without an 'Unmapped' row. Relevant Columns: Participant_Id, Gender, Workshop_Batch_Number, Educational_Stream_Category, Educational_Stream, Age_At_Workshop, Workshop_Name
Educational_Stream_Category vs Educational_Stream (two-level education hierarchy) Educational_Stream_Category is the usable rollup with four allowed values, and it is the authoritative column for the breakdown. Educational_Stream is the partially usable detailed stream with ten values and a handful of nulls elsewhere in the table. The schema warns that 'Medical' in the rollup EXCLUDES Pharmacy, while the detailed value is 'Medical / Public Health'. The two spellings must never be merged in one filter. Under this design, Medical-category girls should show only 'Medical / Public Health' (or null), and Pharmacy-stream girls should fall under Arts/Commerce/Science/Pharmacy. The observed crosstab satisfies the Medical rule fully. The Pharmacy rule holds only vacuously because no Batch 14 girl lists Pharmacy. Relevant Columns: Educational_Stream_Category, Educational_Stream
prod_nirman_questionnaire (explicitly NOT used) The questionnaire holds Pre/Post psychometrics. It has one row per (Participant_Id, Workshop_Level, Workshop_Phase), covers batches 7–17 only, and covers just 2,177 participants. The question forbids a join. This keeps the analysis away from the effective-N shrinkage and skew that the schema warns about, so the 81 baseline is the full demographic population of Batch 14 girls rather than the subset who completed surveys. Relevant Columns: Participant_Id (join key, cast to string, not used here)
