Framing the claim so a downstream check can confirm it

1. The principle

A downstream LLM or analysis checks whether your sentences match the data. Claims fail when they are:

  • Too precise (exact odds ratios, rupee amounts, "63%"), because small differences in cleaning, such as capping, zeros or the 827 vs 828 count, flip a pass to a fail.

  • Causal ("the workshop caused…").
  • Unverified (the mentor lead, since Steps 2–5 returned no results).

So the fix is to state direction and majority instead of exact numbers, use hedged verbs ("tends to", "is associated with"), and label the mentor item as a hypothesis, not a finding.
