2 2. Grounded & Clarified Query (Refined Query)
Disambiguated & Entity-Mapped Query:

"Validate, using the Questionnaire table (prod_nirman_questionnaire), the claim that a higher Pre-workshop Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30 predicts a larger drop (reduction) in Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30 from Pre to Post. Scope: Point-1 workshop only (Workshop_Level == 0.1, parseFloat comparison). Steps: (1) Cast Participant_Id to string. (2) Set 0 to NaN in Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30, then clip to 5,000–1,000,000 INR (or alternatively drop values outside that range) to handle free-typed outliers. (3) Split rows by Workshop_Phase ('Pre' / 'Post') and inner-merge on Participant_Id within the same Workshop_Level, with suffixes _pre and _post. (4) Compute Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30_delta = Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30_post − Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30_pre. Define reduction = 1 if delta < 0, else 0, and also keep drop_amount = pre − post. (5) After dropNa, report the effective N and the share of participants with a reduction. (6) Test the claim: compute Spearman correlation and Pearson correlation (also on log-transformed income) between Financial_Expected_Income_Age_30_pre and drop_amount, and fit a logistic regression of reduction on the Pre value. Compare the Pre value by reduction group (median and quantiles, Mann-Whitney test). Compare the reduction rate and median drop across Pre-income quartiles. (7) Control for regression to the mean and the mechanical ceiling effect, because delta = post − pre is correlated with pre by construction. Do this by comparing the percentage change ((pre − post)/pre) and by regressing Post on Pre (slope < 1 indicates regression to the mean). (8) Optionally check whether other Pre-phase predictors add signal beyond the Pre income value: Life_Criteria_Financial_Security, Life_Criteria_Cause_Of_Work, Career_Plan_Social_Action_Engagement, Metric_Life_Satisfaction_Global, Workshop_Batch_Number, and demographic Gender and Age_At_Workshop (> 0), joined on Participant_Id with the effective N reported. Conclude whether the answer is supported, partially supported, or not supported. Report this as association only, not as evidence that the workshop caused the change, since the data is single-arm observational."

3 3. Answer to Your Question (Summary • Data • Details)
Plain-English Executive Summary (TL;DR) 4/5 Methods Converged (80%)

Higher Pre expected income at age 30 is associated with a larger Pre-to-Post drop and a higher chance of reduction. The association is positive, statistically significant and directionally robust (N=1,313 clipped pairs; 62.99% reduced; Spearman rho≈0.69; raw Pearson r≈0.87; Mann-Whitney rank-biserial≈0.47; reduction rate rises from about 33% in Q1 to about 88% in Q4). Much of it is mechanical coupling and regression to the mean (post-on-pre slope≈0.27 raw, ≈0.5 log), and other Pre predictors add no signal. The claim is supported as an association only, with no causal inference.
