The workshop asks participants to fill in a survey before (Pre) and after (Post). The survey covers "internal change" themes such as:

  • purpose and direction

  • With 11 themes and two genders, some differences will look "significant" by chance, like flipping many coins and being surprised by a streak.

  • The bigger gaps in values, money attitudes and citizenship are not firm. Their groups are small, and men started higher on values (3.46 vs 3.14) and citizenship (3.78 vs 3.55).

  d.Workshop_Batch_Number               AS batch,

3. Step 2: Change by theme and gender

Plain-English idea: for each person, compute a theme score (the average of that theme's items) at Pre and at Post. The delta is Post minus Pre. Then summarise by gender.

Query 2.1: Theme scores per person

A theme score needs at least 70% of its items answered. With 3 placeholder items, that means at least 3 (0.7 × 3 = 2.1, rounded up). The AVG ignores missing values automatically.

sql
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW theme_mvh AS   -- "meaning vs happiness" (example)
SELECT pid, gender, stream_raw, batch, age,
  CASE WHEN (SELECT COUNT(x) FROM (VALUES (a_pre),(b_pre),(c_pre)) v(x)) >= 0.7*3
       THEN (SELECT AVG(x)   FROM (VALUES (a_pre),(b_pre),(c_pre)) v(x)) END AS pre_score,
  CASE WHEN (SELECT COUNT(x) FROM (VALUES (a_post),(b_post),(c_post)) v(x)) >= 0.7*3
       THEN (SELECT AVG(x)   FROM (VALUES (a_post),(b_post),(c_post)) v(x)) END AS post_score
FROM paired;

Query 2.2: Within-gender change summary

sql
SELECT
  gender,
  COUNT(*)                                                   AS n,
  ROUND(AVG(pre_score)::numeric, 2)                          AS pre_mean,
  ROUND(AVG(post_score)::numeric, 2)                         AS post_mean,
  ROUND(AVG(post_score - pre_score)::numeric, 2)             AS mean_delta,
  ROUND(100.0 * AVG((post_score > pre_score)::int), 1)       AS pct_improved,
  ROUND(100.0 * AVG((post_score = pre_score)::int), 1)       AS pct_unchanged,
  ROUND(100.0 * AVG((post_score < pre_score)::int), 1)       AS pct_declined,
  -- paired effect size "dz": average change in units of how much changes vary
  ROUND((AVG(post_score - pre_score) / NULLIF(STDDEV_SAMP(post_score - pre_score),0))::numeric, 2) AS dz,
  -- approximate paired t statistic (look up p-value outside SQL)
  ROUND((AVG(post_score - pre_score) /
        (STDDEV_SAMP(post_score - pre_score) / SQRT(COUNT(*))))::numeric, 1) AS t_stat
FROM theme_mvh
WHERE pre_score IS NOT NULL AND post_score IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY gender;
